Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to print my ID badge?

No, printing is not required. You can simply display the downloaded PDF or a screenshot of your QR code on your smartphone screen at the entry gates.

Is my profile data secure?

Absolutely. Our system uses localized, encrypted databases. Your QR code is a secure 16-byte cryptographically generated token that contains no raw personal data, meaning your identity is safe even if someone screenshots your badge.

What if I lose my QR code?

Don't worry! Visit the Help Desk at the venue. Our staff can instantly look up your name or mobile number in the Nova Ops Command Center and re-issue your badge.

Can the same QR code be used twice for lunch?

Each activity has its own limit that organisers set, either per day or for the whole event, so a second lunch scan can be declined.

How do we add a whole team at once?

Admins upload a simple CSV file and profiles with access tokens are generated instantly for entire corporate teams.

Do our staff need special scanner hardware?

No. The web-based HTML5 QR scanner turns any mobile device into a gate checkpoint, with audio beeps, haptic feedback and instant database sync.
